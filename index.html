What it reads

The # metadata header (session, platform, operator, factorial condition, commanded values, chrony state), then any subset of the v2 schema's 182 columns — power, flight profile, vibration, EKF, wind, loop timing and per-message staleness. Legacy 41-column logs still load: units are normalised on the way in, so mm, cm/s and degrees×10⁷ all arrive as metres, m/s and degrees. The platform is matched from the header or the filename.

What it computes

A GO / REVIEW / HOLD verdict first, then energy, endurance against the pack's rated watt-hours, specific power, and where the charge went by phase. All three power channels are cross-checked against each other and against the autopilot's own energy counter; cadence, staleness, vibration, rails and link errors decide whether the numbers can be trusted at all.

How phases are assigned

Assigned per sample from altitude above launch and smoothed climb rate: ground below 1 m, climb above +0.4 m/s, descent below −0.4 m/s, hover when level and slower than 1 m/s, cruise otherwise. Thresholds are deliberately blunt — for multirotors the hover/cruise split is the one to treat with care, since a slow repositioning move reads as hover.
